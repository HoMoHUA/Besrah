# Besrah — Switch B

## Idea

An original geometric **B**, drawn as two connected transit loops rather than a train, map pin or Persian letter. The open lower interchange and two terminal dots make the letter behave like a route. The meaning comes from the English **Besrah** name and the product action: choosing a better connection through the city.

The avatar intentionally contains no text: the symbol must work at 32 px as well as 1024 px. Its foreground remains inside the Telegram circular crop. The editable vector is the master; PNG/JPEG exports are raster delivery assets.

## Palette

- Midnight teal: `#092D2E` — avatar, navigation, strong typography.
- Electric citron: `#D7FF63` — mark and primary action accent.
- Chalk: `#F3F5EF` — app canvas.
- Deep ink: `#132D2E` — body copy on light surfaces.

Use citron as an accent, not low-contrast body text on white. Keep the mark's internal proportions and intentional lower gap. Do not add gradients, a map pin, a train pictogram, a white square border, tiny Persian text or decorative sparks to the avatar.
