# Static bus guide completeness audit

Scope: every **92 exact imported line refs / 180 retained variants** in the preserved OSM database; source base `2026-10-29T22:19:55Z`. This is **not** a current official operating-line census. No external requests, source corrections, inferred aliases, production changes, or timing work were performed. All results are derived research evidence. Preserve **© OpenStreetMap contributors / ODbL-1.0** when redistributing derived data.

## Findings

- **Names and ordered boarding objects are substantially present.** All 180 variants have names. All **5,275 ordered stop occurrences (2,096 unique linked OSM elements)** have names and valid point coordinates. All **3,243 platform occurrences (1,150 unique linked elements)** have names and literal Point geometries. Every variant has at least 3 stop members, 1 platform member and 13 road segments. Stop sequences are contiguous and agree with preserved relation-member indices; this verifies storage consistency, **not real-world/operator stop order**.
- **One exact raw-endpoint gap:** line `42`, `osm:relation:5815754`, has both `from` and `to` NULL. No names were extracted from its display label to fill these fields.
- **Unlinked inventory is different from route completeness.** There are 100 unlinked stops and 60 unlinked platforms. Across the full DB, 15 stops and 19 platforms lack names. One stop, `osm:way:51881417` / «پایانه زکریا», has no point coordinate; its actual facility geometry is present. Fifteen platforms have nonpoint LineString geometries. All these name/point gaps are outside imported route memberships; no centroid was fabricated.
- **Stop/platform pairing is not available.** Counts differ in 178 variants; that is not proof of missing boarding places. Keep the two object categories separate and preserve raw member indices. Four variants repeat their first stop at the end (`EXP-9`, `EXP-8`, `84`, `83`); those visits must remain separate. Line `33` has two variants with identical ordered stop IDs but nonidentical complete member lists; do not deduplicate them.
- **Road rendering must remain segmented.** Of 11,644 road-segment occurrences, **35 variants across 23 lines** have more than one component when connecting segments by shared exact coordinate vertices. Endpoint-only connectivity finds 36 variants; the difference matters because some ways meet at interior vertices. In source member order, 100 variants are not continuous in original way orientation. Across 11,464 adjacent segment pairs: 10,940 original end→next start joins; 429 other endpoint matches; 4 shared-vertex/no-endpoint matches; 91 pairs with no shared coordinate vertex. Even allowing independent segment reversal for a diagnostic, 38 variants cannot form an uninterrupted endpoint chain in member order. These are distinct, overlapping diagnostics—not confirmed road closures or 100 bad routes. No reversal, stitching, snapping or connector was produced. One-way rules, turn restrictions and independent trip orientation remain unverified.
- **Direction pairing needs review, not automatic completion.** Exact raw endpoint reversal is observed for 156 variants; 18 variants on 11 exact refs have no match: `1093`, `1097`, `205`, `38.1`, `38/1`, `42`, `50`, `60`, `73`, `98.1`, `EXP-4`. Another variant lacks raw endpoints, and five have loop-like evidence. Different terminal-side labels, single directions, punctuation differences and duplicates can explain missing exact matches. `EXP-4` / `osm:relation:13021173` has equal raw endpoint labels but no `roundtrip` tag and different first/last stop elements; this is **evidence to review, not a verified loop**. `38.1` and `38/1` remain distinct. Line `50` retains raw «جرم مطهر» exactly; it was not corrected or equated with another endpoint.
- **Reference hints are quarantined.** There are **74 unmatched platform route-ref lookup tokens**, backed by 1,098 raw token observations. Comparison trims separator whitespace only; internal whitespace, punctuation and prefixes are not repaired. In particular, observed `4` is not an alias for imported `EXP-4`. Forty stop-code lookup tokens refer to multiple OSM stop elements and are not safe unique/provider IDs. Supplementary catalogue/PDF rows remain outside the imported line inventory.
- **Terminal labels are not certified terminal mappings.** There are 39 facility candidates: 19 municipal-operator-tagged and 20 unclassified. Of 360 raw endpoint occurrences, 210 exactly match a named municipal facility candidate; 148 nonempty labels have no exact facility-name match and two are NULL. An unmatched label need not name a terminal at all. No fuzzy/nearest matching or urban classification upgrade was made.
- **Additional structure observations:** lines `99` and `13.1` contain the same non-bus relation member, and line `1098` contains a blank-role node not represented as a stop/platform/road segment. Existing master `osm:relation:13054499` has raw ref `20` but two retained children have raw ref `19`. Twenty variants contain 32 stop occurrences without an exact coordinate match to any own-route road vertex; off-road stop tagging can be intentional, so this is not automatically a geometry gap.
- **Timing/provider boundaries verified:** arrival observations and external stop mappings remain empty; all route headways remain NULL and timing quality remains `unknown`. Piroozi 24 (`osm:node:264158011`) has raw memberships `98`, `91`, `1093`; no consumer-display route membership or provider identity was added.

## Machine-readable outputs

- `summary.json`: explicit metric definitions and overall results; `baseline.json`: full-table SQL null/availability counts and source metadata.
- `route_metrics.json`, `line_metrics.json`: all exact variant/line identities, raw labels, stop/platform availability, repetition, reference observations and review flags.
- `route_geometry_metrics.json`, `geometry_diagnostics.json`: all variants, exact connectivity definitions, component member indices and every non-original-end→next-start adjacent pair.
- `direction_audit.json`: all 180 variants with exact endpoint partners, same-label variants and raw loop evidence.
- `stop_platform_gaps.json`: every full-inventory name/point/geometry gap with affected route/occurrence identifiers; imported route occurrence gap lists are empty.
- `unmapped_ref_hints.json`: unmatched raw tokens, platform evidence and stop-code collisions; `terminal_candidates.json`: all facilities and all 360 endpoint assessments.
- `variant_structure_audit.json`, `structure_observations.json`, `stop_road_vertex_incidence.json`: preserved variants/member anomalies, identifier cases and exact-coordinate-only stop/road observations.
- `verification.json`: independent saved-output coverage/SQL checks, SQLite integrity, SHA-256 before/after, and artifact hashes.

## Static guide implication

The existing snapshot supplies names and point-backed ordered stops/platforms for every imported variant. The concrete missing raw labels, unverified real-world order/direction, segmented geometry, unresolved identifiers and noncertified terminal associations must remain explicit. The audit does not justify repairing source values, claiming coverage of all active Mashhad lines, combining stop/platform objects, offering continuous trip geometry, or presenting live service information.
